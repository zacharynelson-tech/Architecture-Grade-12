How a Cycle Works

1

Read & Reflect

Guided reading notes and a short reflection check-in ground you in the material.

2

Practice

Vocabulary, software, and design-fundamentals work, each graded against a clear rubric.

3

Model in Revit

Revit is your production tool from the start of the year.

4

Reflect & Submit

Work gets submitted through your class's usual channel, linked from each assignment.

Semester 2 Design Studio

Semester 1 teaches you steel, concrete, and mass timber construction. In Semester 2 you apply what you learned. Over three design projects you act as your own small firm, and each project is built around a different structural system.

Project 1 - Steel

Teacher-set brief

Your first studio project: a steel-frame building with a brief and site provided for you.

Project 2 - Concrete

Choose from a menu

Your second studio project: a concrete building, with a program and site you choose from a list.

Project 3 - Mass Timber

Student-written brief

Your third studio project: a mass timber building, with a brief you write and get approved.
