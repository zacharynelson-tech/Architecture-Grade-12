Steel, concrete, masonry, and mass timber: each material changes what a building can be.

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain control layers, how a curtain wall differs from a load-bearing wall, and the pros and cons of steel, concrete, masonry, and heavy/mass timber construction.

Apply

Apply what you know to design a structure in each material, size a mechanical room using rules of thumb, and explain why stacking restrooms is efficient.

Create

Create custom Revit assemblies (roof, exterior wall, floor), a detailed wall section, and a presentation rendering.

Lesson Outline

▸
  • Assembly Basics
    Control layers (water, air, thermal, vapor), curtain walls vs. load-bearing walls, and fenestration.
  • Steel Construction
    Structural steel framing, open web truss, wide flange and castellated beams. Pros and cons and effects on design.
  • Concrete Construction
    Cast-in-place, pre-cast, and pre-stressed concrete. Pros and cons and effects on design.
  • Masonry Construction
    CMU and brick, cavity walls. Pros and cons and effects on design.
  • Heavy Timber / Mass Timber
    Heavy timber, Glu-Lam, cross laminated timber, and dowel laminated timber. Pros and cons and effects on design.
  • MEP Systems
    Means of heating and cooling (VAV, boiler, air handler, chiller), plumbing (vent stack, riser diagrams), electrical. Rules of thumb for mechanical rooms and why stacking restrooms helps.
  • Acoustics & Lighting
    Reverberation, sound absorption, sound isolation, diffusion. Natural vs. artificial light, lighting levels, and controls.
  • Revit Studio Day
    Custom assemblies for roof, exterior wall, and floor: layers, materials, and type properties.
  • Revit Studio Day (cont.)
    Wall section detailing: cut profile, detail lines, patterns, visibility graphics; drafting views.
  • Revit Studio Day (cont.)
    Material browser, rendering properties, and a presentation rendering.
  • Wrap-Up
    Finalize assemblies, section, and rendering. Review for the cycle and for DSA I.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
