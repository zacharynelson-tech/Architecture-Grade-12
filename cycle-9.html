Cycle 9 - Architecture Portfolio, Employment & Marketing

Your work, your resume, and how you present yourself are what get you in the door.

12.8.112.8.212.8.312.8.412.9

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain the purpose and components of an architectural portfolio, the documents and etiquette of job applications and interviews, and effective networking.

Apply

Select materials for a portfolio, write a resume and cover letter, and practice professional conduct including dress and communication.

Create

Create and print a professional portfolio of original work, a resume, a cover letter, and a business card.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

▸
12.8 - Architecture Portfolio
Big Idea(s)
  • Professionalism is a fundamental aspect of an architect's practice, ensuring trust, success, safety, and the ability to make a positive difference in the world through design.
Essential Question(s)
  • Why is it important for architects to exude a professional demeanor?
  • What are some effective strategies for marketing and promoting architectural services, especially for emerging professionals?

Lesson Outline

▸
