Cycle 6 - Commercial Energy Efficiency

Architects have a significant role in how much energy buildings need. Climate, code, and design choices all matter.

12.6.112.6.212.6.312.9

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain why buildings need to be energy efficient, how R-value and U-value relate, and how Net Zero and Passive House differ from the IECC.

Apply

Determine a site's climate zone, apply IECC requirements to a design, and use sustainability principles in the design process.

Create

Create an energy summary for a commercial building comparing assemblies and strategies.

Lesson Outline

▸
  • Why Energy Efficiency
    Major energy uses in buildings and how sustainable design reduces consumption.
  • Climate Zones & the IECC
    Determining a site's climate zone; IECC general requirements and commercial energy efficiency.
  • R-Value, U-Value & SHGC
    R-value vs. U-value, interpreting R-value levels, and solar heat gain coefficient.
  • Fundamentals of Sustainability
    Location and Transportation, Sustainable Site, Water Efficiency, Energy and Atmosphere, Materials and Resources, Indoor Environmental Quality, Carbon Sequestration. Cost savings of green strategies and the best phase to apply them.
  • Beyond Code
    Passive House and Net Zero, and how each differs from the IECC.
  • Studio Work Day
    Apply the IECC to one of your assemblies and propose strategies.
  • Wrap-Up
    Finish your energy summary. Review for the cycle.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
