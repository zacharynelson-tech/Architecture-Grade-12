Cycle 7 - Construction Documents I - General Information, Civil & Structural

Construction documents are the culmination of design, and the contractor has to be able to price and build from them.

12.7.112.7.212.7.312.9

Learning Objectives

Understand

Identify the components of the cover sheet, the project manual, and specification types, and explain what construction documents show and do not show.

Apply

Read and interpret civil and landscape drawings and structural drawings (foundation plan, framing plan, details).

Create

Create a cover sheet and code sheet and an annotated read-through of a civil and structural set.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

▸
12.7 - Construction Documentation
Big Idea(s)
  • Construction Documentation is the culmination of the design phase.
  • A clear and concise construction document is crucial for pricing and constructing the building.
Essential Question(s)
  • How are Construction Documents utilized in the field?

Lesson Outline

▸
  • How CDs Are Used
    How construction documents are used in the field and why they matter for pricing and building.
  • General Information
    Cover sheet, code sheet, specifications, and project manual. Types of specifications: proprietary, performance, prescriptive, relative. MasterFormat divisions.
  • Shop Drawings
    Purpose and content of shop drawings; what construction documents do and do not illustrate.
  • Civil & Landscape Drawings
    Survey, site demo and prep plan, erosion control plan, site layout plan, site utility plan, landscaping plan, and details.
  • Structural Drawings
    Foundation plan, framing plan(s), and structural details.
  • Studio Work Day
    Build a cover sheet and code sheet for a project.
  • Wrap-Up
    Review for the cycle.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
